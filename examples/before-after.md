# Before / After

These are rewrites, not softer wraps. Copy the After. Do not ship the Before with a footnote glued on.

A diff that keeps the Before on a hero, CTA, price, empty state, checkout line, or wiki title **FAILS THE TASK**.

## AI product landing

**Before**
```
Same scene. A new lead. Results vary with your photo.
Checked 2026-10-29 02:43:03 UTC. Availability is checked again in the studio before you buy or generate.
AI-generated preview. Output is not guaranteed and is for reference only.
```

**After**
```
Same scene. Your photo in the lead. Play both to compare.
```

Deep help, once, only if the limit is real: `Use one clear subject and even light.`
Studio, once: `If a template is down for maintenance, you see that before you pay.`

## Pricing

**Before**
```
Starter — $9
Might not include every template. Success is not guaranteed. Prices subject to change.
不保证能出片。结果因人而异。仅供参考。
```

**After**
```
Starter — $9
40 credits. One credit, one render.
```

Tax, if it is real, one line under the grid: `Tax calculated at payment.` Not under every card.

## Checkout guilt

**Before**
```
I have the rights to this photo.

I understand results may vary.
I understand AI output is not guaranteed and is for reference only.
I understand this was not verified for my photo.
结果因人而异。本内容由AI生成，不代表最终效果。
```

**After**
```
12 credits · one 15s video
I have the rights to this photo. I agree to the Terms.
```

One consent. No second checkbox. No variance guilt. No AI-disclaimer spam beside the pay button. Warranty and refunds stay in Terms.

## Game wiki — "not verified"

**Before**
```
# Unverified codes (not tested)

We have not tested these codes in-game. Not verified by us. We cannot confirm they work.
For reference only. These codes might not work.
未经验证。未经测试。不代表一定可用。仅供参考。请自行核实。
```

**After**
```
# Active codes · checked 2026-10-23

STAR-20 — 20 pulls
MOON-5 — 5,000 coins
```

When the list is empty:

```
No active promo codes as of 2026-10-23.
```

Footer, once, not in the H1: `Sources: official news, Destructoid, PGG.`
